About ERMJT Construction

ERMJT Construction is a trusted name in the construction industry, led by R. Marimuthu, DCE, B.Tech. Based in Nallamur, Chengalpattu district, we bring together technical expertise, honest workmanship, and a client-first approach to deliver homes and buildings that stand the test of time.

From drawing and planning to final handover, our team manages every stage of your project with precision and accountability. We offer complete, end-to-end services including construction work, interior work, real estate, documentation work, and all drawing & planning work, ensuring our clients experience a smooth, transparent, and reliable building journey from foundation to finish.

At ERMJT Construction, we understand that every project is unique and every client has a different vision. Our team works closely with clients to understand their requirements, budget and preferences, providing practical solutions that balance functionality, aesthetics and long term value.

  • Complete support from planning and design to final handover
  • Solutions tailored to your needs, budget, and vision
